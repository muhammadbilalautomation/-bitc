# BITC Public Knowledge Base

Source: https://www.bitc.co.bw/
Retrieved: 2026-08-22

This is a concise public-source reference for Thabo’s chat context. It is not a substitute for live browsing or official confirmation.

## Public navigation and service areas

The official BITC website publicly presents the following areas: Home, Invest, Export, BOSSC (Botswana One Stop Service Centre), About BITC, Brand Botswana, and Contacts. It also links to a Procurement Portal, Careers, and a Trade Portal.

## Publicly presented support areas

The website publicly references exporter development and promotion programmes, including the Botswana Exporter Development Programme and Botswana Exporter Promotion Programme (BEPP). It also references Global Expo Botswana, trade agreements, investment climate information, international relations and rankings, investment sectors, and Botswana One Stop Service Centre services.

## Publicly presented institutional focus

The website describes BITC’s public-facing work as focused, selective and targeted investment promotion, effective stakeholder engagement, research, and Brand Botswana / Pride Mark activities. It also provides public pathways to enquire about a business with BITC, register a business, submit feedback, and view contact details.
