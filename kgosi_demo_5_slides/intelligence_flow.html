03 / Intelligence Flow

JARVIS connects the dots to support your decisions.

Example Scenario
“JARVIS, why is our project delivery delayed this month?”
STAGE 01
Command Understanding
JARVIS Action Identifies the intent (Project Delay) and the relevant entities (Current Month, All Active Projects).
STAGE 02
Cross-System Investigation
JARVIS Action Searches Project Management for timelines, Procurement for material delays, and HR for staff shortages.
STAGE 03
Reasoning & Evidence
JARVIS Action Connects a late shipment in Procurement to a milestone miss in Projects. Explains the "Why" with data.
STAGE 04
Decision Support
JARVIS Action Suggests a solution (Alternative Supplier), prepares a briefing, and waits for human approval to act.
From raw data to actionable intelligence
