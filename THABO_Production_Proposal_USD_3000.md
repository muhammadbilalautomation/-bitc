## 1. Executive Summary

THABO is a voice-enabled assistant designed to help users communicate with a business website, conduct research, manage meetings, organise information and support communication tasks. A working prototype has already been prepared using free or limited service plans. The prototype demonstrates the intended user experience and confirms the direction of the system.

This proposal covers the next phase: preparing THABO for a more reliable production deployment using the appropriate paid service plans, completing the required integrations, testing the main workflows, and handing the system over to the client.

> The USD 3,000 fee is for development, integration, deployment, testing, handover and initial support. Third-party subscriptions and usage charges are separate client expenses.

## 3. Production Version to Be Prepared

After the required paid service plans and client access permissions are available, THABO will be configured for a more reliable live environment. The production version will preserve the approved prototype experience while strengthening the connections behind it.

### Voice and conversation

THABO will provide voice and text interaction through the website. The voice service will be connected through the approved ElevenLabs account and configured for the agreed assistant voice and conversation behaviour.

### Calendar management

Through the approved Google Calendar connection and automation workflow, THABO will be prepared to check availability, schedule meetings, reschedule meetings, cancel meetings and confirm meeting details. Calendar actions should remain subject to the client’s chosen approval rules.

### Research and information collection

THABO will be configured to conduct live web research when requested, collect relevant information, summarise the findings and present the results to the user. Research results can be organised and saved to the approved Google Sheets destination for future reference.

### Google Sheets information lookup

Where the client provides an approved structure and access permission, THABO will be able to search stored records in Google Sheets. For example, it can help locate a company or contact record and use the available information in a requested communication workflow.

### Gmail support

THABO will be prepared to create professional email drafts using the information supplied by the user and the approved records. Direct sending can be enabled only according to the client’s approval policy. For safety, external emails should normally require human confirmation before they are sent.

### Automation workflow

The approved n8n workflows will connect the voice assistant with the calendar, research, spreadsheet and email services. The workflows will be configured around the agreed commands and tested individually before handover.

### Multilingual support

The production phase will include the planned support assessment and configuration for Setswana, Shona, Swahili, Ndebele, Zulu and Afrikaans. The final voice quality and language availability will depend on the capabilities and access limits of the selected voice, language and artificial-intelligence providers. Any language that is not fully supported by a provider will be clearly identified before final acceptance.

## 6. Safety and Approval Controls

THABO will assist with requested actions, but sensitive actions should be controlled by the client. Calendar cancellations, external email sending, bulk communication and other actions that create an external consequence should require human confirmation unless the client formally approves an alternative rule.

The system will not be presented as fully autonomous without agreed safeguards. If a provider is unavailable, a permission is denied or information is incomplete, THABO should report the limitation instead of confidently claiming that the action was completed.

## 7. Delivery and Acceptance

The production handover will be considered ready when the agreed workflows have been connected, the main test commands have been completed, the website is accessible through the approved deployment link, and the client has received the basic handover guidance.

The client will provide test accounts, sample calendar requirements, approved research examples, a Google Sheets structure, email rules, language priorities and the required paid service access. Any delay in receiving these items will move the delivery timeline accordingly.

The final production behaviour will also depend on the uptime, limits, permissions and policies of the external providers selected by the client.
